# Inland 47
The Inland 47 is a ortho [planck](/boards/planck)-like keyboard with only one bottom row option, for a total of 47 keys including one center 2u bar. 

It is notable for being one of the few 40%s sold and branded by a major retail store. As of 23 Oct 2026, it is also one of the cheapest pre-built 40%s on the market at $39.99 USD.

## Overview (or History depending on context)
Inland is considered to be the in-house brand of Microcenter, a technology retail brand found in the United States.

The keyboard by default contains Huano Red linears. The keyset is specifically designed for the Inland 47.

## Availability
Only Microcenter stores and online platforms that have their items have them for sale.
