# The NE

The NE is a meme macropad envisioned by dingusxmcgee based on the memery of softsama. It is a 2 key macropad and was brought to life by dingusxmcgee via a small instock "groupbuy" of 25 units in 2023, featuring anodized aluminum cases in red and black, a brass weight and a custom USB C pcb designed by tominabox.
