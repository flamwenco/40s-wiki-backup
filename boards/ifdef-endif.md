# IFDEF ENDIF
IFDEF ENDIF is a split version of [Pragma](/boards/pragma). It utilizes a 'perimeter oring mount' designed by dingus and a custom dual pcb with onboard USB C. It features a tasteful front lip, low front height, subtle undercut curved side profile, and a shadow line running along the entire outside edge.

## Availability

### Private Buys

There was 1 small prototype run in 2026 by dingus of 3 cases, 2 silver and 1 black. There were no plans for a larger run. Dingus just really wanted a split minivan, and Pragma was one of his favorite creations, so he split it in half.
