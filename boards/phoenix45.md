# Phoenix45
Phoenix45 is a 40% keyboard envisioned by projectcain as a rebirth of the classid JD45, with cases made by dingusxmcgee and P3D, and a pcb by Taylor. The premium version was nearly 4lbs with the included massive brass weight and features WKL or HHKB style tops, with a top mounted plate.

## Availability

### Public Groupbuys
Multiple versions of the Phoenix45 were sold in 2020 and 2021, including a premium aluminum and brass version, a 3d printed case and an acryilic case. The boards were sold by a mixture of P3D and MechVault(projectcain).


### Open Source Cases
There is an open source 3d print case by dingusxmcgee [here](https://github.com/dingusxmcgee/Phoenix-45)

### Spinoff PCBs

Alpha stagger drop in pcb [here](https://github.com/galvy0/phoenix45_alpha)

Ortho drop in pcb by Checkerboards.xyz(nasp) (no longer available)


## Timeline
According to projectcain:

 - Cain likes JD45's and has been using them exclusively for years
 - Cain finds the 40% server one day and realizes that people are making their own pcbs here
 - Cain commissions Taylor to re-create the JD45 pcb for the old cases since the JD45 pcbs are outdated and not available anywhere
 - Cain names the new pcb the Phoenix for obvious reasons and launches an IC for JD45 owners only
 - After seeing a lot of interest in the channel (from non-JD45 owners) dingusxmgee selflessly volunteers to design a case to fit the Phoenix pcb
 - dingus' design is kicked over to Jake from P3Dstore to make the 3DP case. After a few prototypes (like the beige one) some tweaks are made and everything is ready to go
 - Jake has the case in-stock for a while, and PCBs are available from Cain via google form
 - Popularity increases and Cain decides to let Jake take over the production and sale of the PCBs
 - Jake starts making acrylic cases to go with his 3DP versions
 - Cain gets a couple dozen messages about bringing a metal phoenix case to GB, but doesn't have any design skills yet.
 - Cain commissions dingusxmgee to adapt his 3DP case to a CNC design while changing up the bottom. 
 - IC/GB was launched and here we are
