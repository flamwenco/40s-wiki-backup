# Keycap Manufacturers

Mass production of keycaps requires specific manufacturing equipment including machines for injection molding, sorting, and packaging. The high barrier to entry for this scale of production means there are only a few well established manufacturers that have consistently delivered high quality keycaps for the enthusiast community.
