# PFF Profile

PFF stands for "Penguin Flat Feet". It is a [keycap profile](/en/keycaps/profiles) "designed specifically for low profile switches."[^1]

Designed by [Matt3o](/en/designers/matt3o) and manfactured by [Keyreative](/en/keycaps/manufacturers/keyreative).
